# SafeGround user guide

A calm companion for finding help, resting, and staying connected to people
who care. Everything here is opt-in. Nothing here watches you. Take your time
— every screen waits for you.

## For neighbors

**Find help (`/help`).** The Resource Navigator lists nearby food, shelter,
clinics, and more — with a map, details, and directions. Everything is open to
read: no account, no permission prompts. If you tap "Use my location once,"
the app reads your location a single time for that one lookup, and nothing is
stored. If listings look saved from an earlier visit while you're offline,
you'll see a gentle banner that says so, with a Retry button.

**Sweep heads-ups (`/sweeps`).** See reported and MPRCC-verified sweep areas
on a list and a map. The list works fully with keyboard and screen readers;
map pins are a visual extra. You can report a sweep you see — it goes to the
outreach team for review, and verified ones carry an "MPRCC Verified" badge.

**Check-ins (`/checkin`).** Let people you trust know you're resting: check in
for the night, and pause sharing anytime — pausing hides you instantly. Your
exact spot is yours alone; trusted peers only ever see an approximate
~150m area, and it fades after 24 hours.

**Trusted peers (`/checkin/peers`).** Invite someone by phone number — they
choose to accept or decline, and either of you can remove the connection
anytime. Only mutual peers can see your approximate area. You can also share
an invite code (6 characters, good for 48 hours) in person instead of typing a
number.

**Get help from my people (`/alerts/new`).** One tap sends an alert to the
people you choose: trusted friends and peers always, plus HomeTeam supporters
if you'd like them included. You pick the kind (unsafe place, police nearby,
or help needed), add a short note if you want, and choose per alert whether to
share no location, an approximate area (~150m), or your exact spot. Exact
spots are seen only by the people notified plus MPRCC staff, and fade when the
alert does. HomeTeam supporters only get alerts during daytime hours
(8am–6pm, Monday–Friday) unless they've asked for after-hours messages.
Sending never calls 911 or any agency — that choice stays with you, always.
You clear your own alert with "I'm OK" when you're safe; alerts also rest
after 24 hours.

**Ask a peer for support (`/peer-support`).** One tap with your phone number —
"an MPRCC peer will reach out." It lands in the outreach team's queue and
notifies the two admins. No sirens, no agencies, no pressure.

**Log a need (`/hometeam`).** Name what you need (a tent, a blanket, a ride)
with your phone number so the team can reach you. You'll get a calm
confirmation card, and the need stays visible until it's claimed and delivered.

## For HomeTeam supporters

**Join (`/hometeam`).** Sign up with your phone and name — no account, no
location. After-hours messages default OFF; you can opt in if you'd like to
be reachable at night.

**Claim and deliver.** The needs feed shows open needs ("Joe needs a tent at
the skatepark"). Tap "I got that" to claim one, and mark it done when it's
delivered. The first claim wins, so nothing gets doubled up. Some needs are
marked for coordinator assignment or outreach-only — those won't show a claim
button, and that's expected.

**Alerts you may receive.** If a neighbor includes HomeTeam in an alert,
you'll get a push during daytime hours (8am–6pm weekdays, unless you've opted
into after-hours). If you can help, tap "I'm on it" so others know someone's
going — and you'll get a "Get directions" button if they shared their exact
spot. The neighbor clears the alert when they're okay.

## For MPRCC staff (outreach dashboard)

**Sign in with your roster phone (`/outreach`).** The dashboard checks your
phone against the outreach roster on every call. Full admins (Jenn + Bambi)
see everything: active alerts with contact details, resolved alerts with
outcomes, sweeps to verify, open needs, and the peer-support count. Limited
staff (Tracey) sees active alerts and open needs plus sweep verify/flag —
names and last-4 digits only, never full phone numbers, never resolved
outcomes or analytics.

**Sweeps:** verify or flag reported sweeps; resolving is admin-only.
**Needs:** claim and mark delivered for the team. **Alerts:** the sender
clears their own alert ("I'm OK") as the usual path; admins can clear as a
safety override when the sender can't — with a short outcome note, which
becomes the record (time-to-resolve, who helped).

**Peer-support queue (`/peer-support-queue`, admin-only).** Open and claimed
requests, newest first. Claim one ("I'm on it"), hand it to a teammate's
phone, or mark it done with an optional outcome note. Limited staff sees a
calm "for the outreach team" message here instead of rows.

**Community directory (`/outreach/directory`, roster-gated).** Admins see the
full roster with contact and consent details; limited staff sees names and
kinds only. Group notices (admin-only composer): pick an audience, write a
headline and a few lines, review the live recipient estimate, and send —
delivery is push-only, daytime-hours, opt-in. Sent history lives under
notices.

**Analytics (`/admin/analytics`, admin-only).** Anonymous counters only —
requests this month, top services, sweep alerts, active users — plus a
"Generate Monthly Impact Report" CSV for grant proposals. No names, no
phones, no locations anywhere on this screen.

**Push self-test (`/push-test`).** Three gentle steps — register this phone,
check the wiring (no delivery), then send yourself a real test — plus the
peer-support fan-out check. iPhones need Add-to-Home-Screen first.
